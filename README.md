# Abstract
The University Grade and Attendance Tracker is a user-friendly system developed to simplify and improve
the management of students' academic records. It helps store and manage important information such as
student details, subject-wise marks, grades, attendance, and overall academic performance in an
organized manner. The system reduces the effort involved in maintaining records manually and minimizes
calculation and data-entry errors.
The tracker allows faculty members to update grades and attendance regularly, while students can
monitor their academic progress and attendance status. It can automatically calculate attendance
percentages, grades, and other performance-related information, making the process faster and more
accurate. By keeping academic information in a centralized system, the project improves accessibility,
saves time, and provides better record management. Overall, the University Grade and Attendance
Tracker offers an efficient and reliable solution for managing academic performance and attendance in a
university environment
